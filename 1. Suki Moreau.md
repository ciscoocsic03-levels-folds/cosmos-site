## Basic Information

Role: Vocals & Songwriter

Band: [[00. The Crimsons Sparks]]
## Description

Suki writes in notebooks she never throws away. Every lyric, every scratch, every crossed-out line. She has been playing small venues since she was nineteen, never chasing the big break because she never trusted what came with it.

She is the kind of performer who makes you feel like she is singing specifically to you, about something you thought only you understood. Her voice carries weight without trying. It's the kind of thing you can't manufacture.

## History

When Soren found her at The Rusty Nail, she was singing a song she wrote about her father leaving. Nobody was listening. She kept singing anyway. That's Suki.

## Personality

The tour changed her. When the band found themselves trapped in a country mid-insurgency, Suki stopped writing about herself. She started paying attention to everything outside her own story - other languages, other losses, other people's versions of the same pain. She came home speaking fragments of Japanese, Afrikaans, Arabic, and German. Not perfectly. Perfectly enough. The notebooks didn't change. What goes in them did.

## Abilities / Strengths

Suki is very down to earth and can think quickly on her feet. She can write sonnets and songs in 5 minutes and is extremely perceptive of her environment

## Quotes
"If it doesn't cost you something, it's not worth singing."
