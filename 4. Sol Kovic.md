Role: Drums

Band: [[00. The Crimsons Sparks]]

## Description

The youngest. Twenty-three years old and she plays like she's been doing this her entire life - because she has. She taught herself on a practice pad in her bedroom because her apartment had thin walls and a drum kit wasn't an option.

She always looks like she is about to move. Drumsticks tucked in her back pocket everywhere she goes. She's the only one of The Crimson Sparks who didn't struggle to be found - she just hadn't been found yet.
## History

Soren heard her at an open mic on a borrowed kit and called Suki that same night. Sol was the last to say yes. She was also the last one still on stage after every show, too wired to leave.

Before The Crimson Sparks, she played drums in a ska-punk band - her first real home. She left on good terms, the kind of leaving that doesn't break things. When "Snow on the Beach" made it onto the second album, Sol called her old crew back in. Horn players, rhythm section, the whole chaotic warmth of her ska roots. They recorded it in two sessions and it sounded exactly like something she would have written at twenty with nowhere to be.
## Personality

Super confident and loud! She gets lost in the music when they perform.

## Abilities / Strengths

Very versatile in terms of music. Can play almost any genre.
## Quotes
"If the drummer isn't having fun, nobody is."
