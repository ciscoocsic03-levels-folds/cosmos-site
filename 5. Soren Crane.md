## Description

He used to be somebody. His old band played stadiums, got magazine covers, won awards. Then the label came with a rebrand deal and everyone shook hands except Soren. He walked out. Said nothing publicly. Just disappeared.
## History

He spent a year in dive bars and late nights wondering if he'd thrown everything away for a principle nobody else seemed to care about. Then he heard Suki sing in The Rusty Nail and he had his answer.

He rarely speaks between songs. When he does the others listen. He has been through the machine and came out the other side with nothing left to prove. That's what makes him dangerous.
## Personality

The silent and wise one. Rarely speaks. But when he does, it's always serious.

## Abilities / Strengths

The Heart and pulse of the band.

"The bass line is the truth underneath everything else."
