## Basic Information

Role: Lead Guitar

Band: [[00. The Crimsons Sparks]]

## Description

Wild curly hair, paint-stained fingers, always wearing something she found at a thrift store that somehow looks like she planned it. She never plays the same solo twice. You watch Suki when the song starts. You watch Sky when it breaks open.
## History

Three bands before The Crimson Sparks. Every one of them fell apart before they got anywhere. She almost quit the second time - sat in her apartment with her guitar case packed and the door open. Suki talked her out of it over cheap coffee at two in the morning.
## Personality

The Bright spark - the fun one in the band. Always sees the positive of everything.

## Abilities / Strengths

Very talented guitar player. Can play almost any string instruments. 

## Quotes
"Rules are for people who haven't heard enough music yet."
