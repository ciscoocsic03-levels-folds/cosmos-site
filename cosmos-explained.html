Levels & Folds Philosophy

Levels and Folds are not chronology. They do not represent Past / Present / Future. Instead: all Levels and all Folds exist simultaneously. Each Fold contains its own inhabitants, stories, civilizations, and systems. All coexist within the same greater Cosmos.

Example hierarchy (illustrative):

Important: all levels and folds exist together — this is not PAST/PRESENT/FUTURE. Think of them like folded space: different songs, artists, lore, culture, technology, religion, characters, etc., may vary by level or fold while coexisting in the same Cosmos.
