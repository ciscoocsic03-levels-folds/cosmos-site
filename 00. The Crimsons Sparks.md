## Maya Era

Eventually:

[[00. Maya]] becomes a major artist.

One night she headlines an enormous concert.

In the crowd:

- [[1. Suki Moreau]] 
- [[2. Sky Delacroix]]
- [[3. Sienna Aramaki]]
- [[4. Sol Kovic]]
- [[5. Soren Crane]]

stand together.

Not backstage.

Not on stage.

Not announced.

Just watching.

The spark has been passed on.

---

## Final Canon Statement**

The Crimson Sparks never perform together again.

Not because they stop loving music.

Not because they stop loving each other.

But because:

The story was complete.

The spark became a fire.

The fire became a wildfire.

The wildfire became embers.

And the embers found new homes.
