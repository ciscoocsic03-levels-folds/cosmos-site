## Description

Her father is one of the most celebrated conductors in the world. Her mother, a cellist of the same tier. Sienna grew up backstage at concert halls, learning what perfection sounded like before she understood what music felt like. She was classically trained from age five. Piano first. Then guitar, quietly, on her own.

She was good enough to earn a full scholarship. She quit it at twenty-two. The institution wanted performance. She wanted creation. That distinction mattered more than the diploma, more than the family name, more than the future they had already mapped out for her.
## History

What pushed her over the edge was a ska band she heard through a window one Tuesday night. Loud, chaotic, off in all the ways the conservatory had taught her to avoid. She stood outside listening until they stopped. She went home. She packed a bag. She left.

She played on street corners for almost a year. That's where she met Suki - both of them busking outside a train station, Suki with a notebook and Sienna with a guitar she'd bought secondhand. They never formally decided to stay in each other's lives. They just did.

Sol was the surprise. When Soren brought the band together, Sienna recognized her immediately - the drummer from that ska band. The one through the window. She never told Sol. She didn't need to. The music already knew.
## Personality

On stage she transforms. Off stage she could be anyone's older sister. Both versions are completely real.

## Abilities / Strengths

Classically Trained and has almost perfect tonality and can hear even the slightest imperfection in any note.

## Quotes
